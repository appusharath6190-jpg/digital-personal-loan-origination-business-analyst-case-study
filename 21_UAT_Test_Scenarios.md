# UAT Plan

## Objective
Confirm the configured workflow supports agreed business processes, control requirements and customer / operations outcomes before release.

## Participants
Product Owner, Lending Operations, Credit Risk, Compliance, Customer Service, Technology, Data / MI and relevant control owners.

## Entry criteria
- Requirements baselined and approved.
- UAT environment and masked / synthetic test data available.
- Test accounts and role permissions configured.
- Dependencies / stubs available and known limitations documented.
- Test scenarios reviewed and expected results agreed.

## Execution
1. Conduct role-based walkthrough.
2. Execute happy-path, negative, boundary and exception scenarios.
3. Capture actual result, evidence, tester, timestamp and defect ID.
4. Triage defects by severity and business impact.
5. Retest fixes and execute regression.
6. Obtain business and control-owner sign-off.

## Exit criteria
- All critical and high-priority scenarios pass or have formally accepted disposition.
- No open critical defect.
- High defects have approved remediation / workaround and owner.
- Audit, access, consent and disbursal controls demonstrated.
- KPI data reconciles to source events for agreed sample.
- Business, Risk, Compliance and Operations approvals recorded.
