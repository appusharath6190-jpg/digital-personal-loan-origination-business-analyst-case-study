# As-Is Process Analysis

## Current-state journey (fictional baseline)
1. Applicant starts an application through a branch, website or mobile channel.
2. Applicant enters personal, employment and income information.
3. Documents are uploaded or collected by an assisted channel.
4. Operations checks completeness and follows up for missing items.
5. KYC status is checked through a separate service / operational screen.
6. Bureau information is requested through an approved process.
7. Credit / operations reviews affordability and policy exceptions.
8. Exceptions are routed to a reviewer, sometimes through manual queues.
9. Decision and offer details are communicated.
10. Applicant accepts terms; agreement and pre-disbursal checks are completed.
11. Disbursal instruction is sent and status is updated.

## Observed / hypothesised pain points to validate
- Repeated entry of customer details across channels and systems.
- Incomplete applications reach operations, increasing rework.
- No single view of pending documents, KYC and verification status.
- Manual follow-ups and exception queues create ageing.
- Applicants cannot distinguish “in progress” from “action required”.
- Policy changes are difficult to trace if rules are embedded in different places.
- Management reporting is delayed or inconsistent.
- Decision overrides may lack standard reason codes or consistent evidence.

## Evidence required during discovery
Baseline volumes; channel mix; application abandonment; rework reasons; KYC failure / pending rates; bureau latency; manual review volumes; decision TAT by segment; exception ageing; offer acceptance; disbursal failures; complaints; audit findings.

Do not present these pain points as measured facts. They are discovery hypotheses for this portfolio simulation.
