# Regulatory Reference Notes (India)

This portfolio case study is designed with regulatory awareness, not as a compliance interpretation or legal opinion. Always verify the latest applicable requirements with the institution's Compliance and Legal teams before implementation.

## Reference points
1. **RBI (Digital Lending) Directions, 2025** – relevant topics include borrower creditworthiness assessment, disclosures, disbursal / servicing / repayment, cooling-off, grievance redressal, data collection, usage and storage. Official RBI notification: https://rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=12848&Mode=0
2. **RBI Master Direction – Know Your Customer (KYC) Direction, 2016** (official RBI page shows updated versions) – customer identification and due diligence requirements. Official RBI page: https://rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=11566
3. **RBI FAQs on KYC** – explains the purpose of KYC and when it is required. Official RBI FAQ: https://www.rbi.org.in/commonman/english/scripts/FAQs.aspx?Id=3782
4. **RBI Key Facts Statement (KFS) for Loans & Advances, April 15, 2024** – standardised key loan facts and APR disclosure for covered retail and MSME term loans. Official RBI notification / circular should be checked for current applicability and amendments.

## BA implications
- Capture consent, purpose, timestamp and wording version where required.
- Define KYC status, exception and evidence requirements with Compliance.
- Capture age, occupation, income and other approved economic-profile information needed for creditworthiness assessment.
- Provide clear loan terms and applicable KFS before acceptance.
- Keep audit evidence for decisions, customer communications and data access.
- Define data minimisation, access, storage, retention and deletion with Privacy / Legal.
- Ensure third-party service providers and lending service provider arrangements are assessed by accountable teams.

## Important
The case study does not claim that every control listed is legally mandated in every product/channel combination. Applicability depends on the regulated entity, lending model, current directions, product and facts. No regulatory requirement should be inferred solely from this sample.
