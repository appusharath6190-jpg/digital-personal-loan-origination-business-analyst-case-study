# Stakeholder Register

| Stakeholder | Interest / responsibility | BA engagement |
|---|---|---|
| Product Owner / Lending Business | Product goals, customer journey, prioritisation | Workshops, scope and sign-off |
| Credit Risk | Eligibility, affordability, policy, exceptions | Rule workshops; approval of policy rules |
| Compliance / AML | KYC, consent, customer treatment, regulatory obligations | Control review and requirement validation |
| Legal | Contract terms, KFS and customer disclosures | Review wording and legal dependencies |
| Operations | Application review, queues, exceptions, disbursal readiness | Interviews, process walkthroughs, UAT |
| Fraud / Financial Crime | Fraud indicators, referrals, investigation workflow | Scenario and escalation workshops |
| Customer Service | Customer status, complaints and servicing hand-off | Journey and notification review |
| Technology / Architecture | APIs, workflow, integration, data and platform constraints | Solution walkthroughs and feasibility |
| Information Security / Privacy | Access, encryption, privacy, retention and monitoring | NFR and control review |
| Data / MI | KPI definitions, reporting and lineage | Data dictionary and dashboard requirements |
| Internal Audit / Risk Control | Evidence, traceability and control assurance | Audit trail and evidence review |
| Applicants / Customers | Clear application, consent, status and offer information | Journey research / usability testing |

## RACI (high-level)
- **Accountable:** Product Owner for product scope; Credit Risk for credit policy; Compliance for compliance controls; Technology for technical design and delivery.
- **Responsible:** BA for elicitation, analysis, documentation, traceability and facilitation; engineering teams for implementation; Operations for process execution.
- **Consulted:** Legal, Fraud, Privacy, Security, Data, Customer Service, Audit.
- **Informed:** Branch / channel teams, leadership and impacted support teams.

The BA facilitates decisions but does not independently approve credit policy, regulatory interpretation or production architecture.
